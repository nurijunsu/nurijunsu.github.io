Hi, I am Junsu Kim, on a journey searching for ideas and people that can make a fundamental change to the world.

Previously, I was a Student Researcher at Google DeepMind's Superhuman Reasoning team, working on post-training and inference-time methods for mathematical reasoning. We won a gold medal 🏅 at the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) with Gemini Deep Think, and built Aletheia, a research agent solving open problems in mathematics.

I'm interested in bringing these reasoning capabilities to real-world reasoning about people and our society, which requires much longer context and careful reward definition. Ultimately, I believe this development can be done in a way that benefits society, preserving human autonomy and self-actualization rather than replacing us — the future I want to help build.

My journey stems from the Ryu Optimization Group, where I first dived into the field of artificial intelligence and learned how to research. Before that, I enjoyed my time preparing for the IMO, where I first learned how to think.

If you'd like to chat, I'm hosting a 30-minute Chat every day. Feel free to connect — I love talking with new people!
